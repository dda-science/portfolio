Project 1 · Voice assistantAll projects

An assistant that phones people who ask for information and leaves their record filled in.

It is a program with a voice. It holds a short conversation, asks the questions it needs and notes down the answers. Nobody on the team has to dial the number.

How it looks inside

Before and now

Before

  • Someone on the team called each request, one by one.
  • Calls could only be made during office hours.
  • After hanging up, they had to enter the details in the record.

Now

  • The assistant calls as soon as the request comes in.
  • It also calls in the evening and at weekends.
  • The person on the team gets the record already filled in.

What I did

  1. I designed the conversation: what the assistant says, what it asks and in what order.
  2. I connected it to the client record so each answer is saved in the right place.
  3. I check it with automated tests before every change.
  4. I review the calls to fix anything that doesn't sound natural.

ResultsClients can be served outside office hours and at weekends, and called the moment they ask for information, instead of waiting for someone on the team to be free.
