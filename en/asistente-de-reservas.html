Project 3 · Booking assistantAll projects

An assistant that answers by chat whether there is availability, with the price, and sends the link to book straight away.

I built it for a group of accommodation providers. It checks availability in their booking system, replies with the free rooms and their prices, and sends the booking link with those dates already filled in. If nothing is free, it suggests other dates. It saves the request and hands the conversation over to a person when needed.

What changes

Someone asking about dates wants to know if there is space, not to wait for someone to check.

For the client

They get an answer with real availability and a link to book straight away. If nothing is free, it suggests other dates.

For the team

They get the request saved in the record, and the conversation when a person is needed.

What I did

  1. I designed the conversation and the tricky cases: booked dates, messages written in all sorts of ways, and handover to a person.
  2. I connected it to the booking system so it replies with real availability and prices.
  3. I made sure every enquiry is saved in the client record.
  4. I tested it with conversation scripts before handing it over.

ResultsIt served real guests at one of the properties, at any time of day.
