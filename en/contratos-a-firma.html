One button in the client record: the contract fills itself in with their details and goes out for signature.

Each blank in the template is linked to a field in the record: name, ID document, address, amount. The person on the team clicks the button, picks the template, checks what will go out and sends it for signature. It is quick and nothing has to be copied by hand.

The real tool

Before and now

Before

  • Each contract was prepared by hand, copying the client's details one by one.
  • Many expired unsigned.
  • The record did not show that it had expired.

Now

  • You choose the template and it fills itself in, with the amount also written in words.
  • There is a review screen before sending.
  • If it expires unsigned, the record shows it.

What I did

  1. I designed the screen: choose a template, review and send.
  2. I linked each blank in the templates to its field in the client record.
  3. I added the mandatory review step before sending.
  4. I made it visible in the record when a contract expires unsigned.
  5. I wrote the guide for adding new templates.
