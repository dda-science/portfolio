Project 2 · Chat assistantAll projects

A chat that understands what the client writes, however they write it.

It collects the details needed to get started by message, in the website chat, on WhatsApp or in whatever channel the company uses. The client answers how and when they like, and each detail is saved cleanly in their record.

The real conversation

What changes

Before, someone on the team had to ask for the details one by one and write them down by hand.

For the client

They answer by message, in their own words and when it suits them.

For the team

They get the details already organised in the record. If something fails when saving, they get a task with all the answers.

What I did

  1. I decided what is asked and which answers are accepted for each question.
  2. I made simple rules apply first, with AI only stepping in when those rules are not enough.
  3. I built a fallback for failures: if the details are not saved, the team gets a task with everything that was answered.
