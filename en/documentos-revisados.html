Project 8 · AI document handlingAll projects

Clients send their paperwork however they can. The office receives it sorted and named, and anything missing is requested automatically.

It works for any business that receives a lot of paperwork, such as an accounting or advisory firm. The client sends photos and files in no particular order. The AI recognises what each one is (an ID document, a payslip, a tax return), straightens it, names it and saves it in that client's record. It then compares what has arrived with that client's details in the CRM and knows which documents are missing for their case. It also reduces file size, so the files can be sent by email without going over the limit.

What I did

  1. I designed the full journey of a document, from when the client sends it until it is in their record.
  2. I decided which document types are recognised and how they are named and grouped.
  3. I tested it with eight complete test-client cases before launching it.
  4. I made sure personal data is not written to the system's internal logs.
