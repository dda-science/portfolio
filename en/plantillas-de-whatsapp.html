Project 5 · WhatsApp template sendingAll projects

A WhatsApp template sent from the CRM, to one contact or a whole list, without anyone receiving it twice.

It works for any business that wants to notify many clients at once: an invitation, a reminder, some news. The team picks the template and who it goes to: a single person, from their record, or a whole list. The tool sends the messages one by one, each with its own personal link, and logs each send in the person's record.

The real tool

What changes

Before, messages were sent one by one and there was no record of who had been contacted. Now the same template is sent to one person or a whole list, without repeating anyone on the same day.

  • Each person gets their own link.
  • Each record shows what was sent, when and by whom.
  • The conversation then continues in the team chat.

What I did

  1. I designed the two screens: send to one person and send to a list.
  2. I made sure it logs what was sent, when and who sent it.
  3. I added a safeguard so the same message is not sent twice on the same day.
  4. I checked it with automated tests and real sends.
