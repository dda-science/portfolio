Project 11 · SMS with a personal formAll projects

A text message with a personal link, and the client completes what is missing on their own.

The client gets an SMS with a link that is theirs alone. When they open it, they find a form that already contains what was known about them, and they only fill in what is missing. It is used to get back in touch with people who could not be reached, to ask for missing details or to update ones that have changed.

The real tool

What changes

Before, every missing detail had to be requested by hand, client by client, and many requests were left half-finished.

For the client

They get a short message with a link. The form already contains their details and they only complete what is missing.

For the team

The record is updated with what the client fills in. The message only goes out when it is allowed to be sent.

What I did

  1. I designed the journey: when the message goes out, what it says and where it leads.
  2. I adjusted the text so it fits in a single message.
  3. I made the link personal, so it cannot be guessed or altered.
  4. I set up a dashboard to track the results and wrote the maintenance manual.
