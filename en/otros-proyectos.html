Other projects

Smaller tools, without their own demo.

  • One request, several banksTo apply for financing with several banks at once. The account manager ticks them in a list and all the requests are created in one go, instead of one by one.
  • History of each requestEvery time a bank changes a request's status, it is recorded with the date, so you know where it stands without asking.
  • Bank file, put together with AIBanks want documents arranged in a particular way. One button prepares them from the client's loose files and flags anything missing.
  • Document requests with templatesAsking a client for paperwork meant writing the same email every time. Now one button sends it with each account manager's and team's template, already filled in with their details.
  • Emailing banks without looking up addressesEach bank has several contacts. From the client record you find the bank, tick the people and their addresses go into the email automatically.
  • Automatic client allocationEach new client gets a priority based on their profile and is assigned to an account manager in turn, without anyone having to allocate them by hand.
  • Income recorded on paymentWhen the company is paid for a deal, the income is recorded automatically and the client, the account manager and the admin team are notified.
  • Forms that already know the clientThe client does not have to type again what they already said: the form arrives with those details filled in, and what they add goes back to their record.
  • Which ad each client came fromYou know which ad brought in each client, and that data goes back to the ad platform so money goes to the ads that work.
  • Opt-outs and data protectionWhen a client asks not to be contacted again, all their scheduled messages and calls stop automatically.
  • Quality of the assistant's callsIt checks how each call ends and measures how many go well. The analysis of the failed calls was used to make a complaint to the phone provider, backed by data.
  • KPI dashboardsDashboards that show at a glance how the business is doing: how many clients come in through each channel, how long the first contact takes and how much workload each account manager has.
  • Revenue figures reviewEach revenue figure was recalculated another way to check it was correct, amounts were reconciled across parts of the system and the incorrect reports were fixed.
  • Team requests, in orderChange requests that used to arrive scattered moved to a single place, where each person notes what they need and can see its status. 44 requests and 18 tasks were organised this way.
  • Daily AI checksEvery morning, automated checks make sure calls, client allocation and ID documents are in order, and flag anything that does not add up.
  • Domain change without downtimeThe company changed its website and email address. Automations, calendars and signature templates were moved over one by one without interrupting work.
  • Contact database clean-upDuplicate contacts and data written in different ways were found and merged.
  • AI for the teamAssistants set up so each person can do tasks on their own that they used to ask someone else to do, such as putting together a file, drafting emails or reviewing calls.
